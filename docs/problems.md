# Competitor Research

## Issues with WO Mic
- Potentially malicious app
- Not enough adjustment settings. Can only adjust volume.
- Has a noticeable ~1 second delay
- Audio quality is capped unless you pay for subscription

## What they did right
- Connection between phone and desktop is relatively quick and easy
- Does support connecting to other output devices (speakers/headphones)
  via Bluetooth/USB. Worth considering as a future feature.

## Issues with KDE Connect
- No microphone feature at all
- Mouse control has noticeable input lag/smoothness issues. Annoying
  for actual use as a primary mouse
- File/photo transfer occasionally glitches or fails
- "Find a phone" feature only works when phone and app is open. Not very effective for real life situations.

## What they did right
- KDE supports many different features. Potentially worth looking into in the future.
- Very quick and automatic connection between phone and desktop.
